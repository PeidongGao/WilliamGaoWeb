Reading the fingerprint

Radar chart

The radar chart shows the nine tracked indicators together. The dashed circle marks no overnight change; positions beyond or within it show the size and direction of each direction-adjusted move. Labels show the raw gap/change percentage.

Heatmap

The heatmap presents the same overnight changes as individual tiles. Green indicates a positive direction-adjusted move and red indicates a negative one. VIX and the U.S. dollar are reversed so the chart reads in a consistent market-direction context.

Hover, focus, or tap a point or tile to inspect its baseline and opening/reference value. Switch between the full figure, radar, and heatmap, or zoom and drag to inspect the original chart more closely.

Methodology and source

The fingerprint is generated automatically by the open-source Daily Market Tracker project.

Each U.S. trading day, the workflow downloads Yahoo Finance market data, calculates the changes, validates and stores the daily record, and generates the fingerprint.

Traditional instruments compare the previous close with their daily open. Bitcoin compares its 00:00 UTC daily open with the current market price at collection time. Treasury-yield changes are relative percentages, not basis points.
